How Technology Makes Me Think

A few technology concepts I find genuinely fun — explained the way I actually think about them, through everyday life rather than jargon.

A Little Intelligent Fun

OOPS, But Make It Real Life

Four ideas from Object-Oriented Programming — hover or tap a card to see the connection

☕

Encapsulation

Real Life

A coffee machine. You press [ Coffee ] — you don't need to know how the water heats or the pressure builds.

The Tech Concept

Hide the internal implementation and expose only what's needed. The machine gives you a button, not a manual.

🌱

Inheritance

Real Life

Family. A child often inherits traits and habits from their parents, then adds a few of their own.

The Tech Concept

A child class inherits properties and behavior from a parent class — and can extend or override them.

Hover or tap
🎭

Polymorphism

Real Life

The same person behaves differently by context — calm at home 😌, focused at work 💻, competitive on the court 🏀.

The Tech Concept

Same interface, different implementation — the action stays the same, but what happens underneath changes.

Hover or tap
🚗

Abstraction

Real Life

Driving a car. You use the steering, brake and accelerator — no need to understand the whole engine.

The Tech Concept

Expose the essential functionality while hiding unnecessary complexity underneath.
